What this does

Scan a grocery barcode. Lettuce Know checks it against live FDA and USDA recalls, then flags ingredients the US and EU regulate differently. Save a product and it gets re-checked every time the data refreshes.

Where the data comes from

  • Recalls: openFDA and USDA FSIS, refreshed twice a day.
  • Products: Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central.

What it can't tell you

Recalls are matched on brand and product name, not the barcode — no public database links the two. So “no recall found” means nothing matched, not that a product is safe.

The EU/US notes say what each regulator allows. They aren't a safety score.

Ingredient flags read label text from the product databases. “May contain” warnings often aren't in that data, so read the package if you have an allergy.

Always check the lot code and date on your own package before deciding.

Spotted a wrong match?

Matching is fuzzy by design, so mistakes happen. Email what you found and it helps make the matching better.

Your data

There's no account and nothing is tracked. Your watchlist, history, and settings live only in this browser — the only thing sent anywhere is the barcode itself, to check it against FDA and USDA data.
